deal.m4a: made from the "card-slide" recordings in Casino Audio by Kenney (www.kenney.nl), Creative Commons Zero
(CC0): the crisp part of each slide, laid out as 14 cards landing about 10 a second with small changes in timing,
pitch and volume, softened (highs rolled off above ~3 kHz, gentle onsets), levelled and converted to AAC.

glass.m4a: "Small Glass Pane Shatter", supplied by Nick for the family game; trimmed to 1.25 s with a short fade-out and converted to AAC.

Animated emojis: Noto Emoji Animation by Google (CC BY 4.0), loaded from fonts.gstatic.com when shown;
  https://googlefonts.github.io/noto-emoji-animation/

night.m4a: from "Campaign at Night #4" (field crickets and rare frogs in a meadow, France, 2020) by Joseph Sardin,
BigSoundBank, https://bigsoundbank.com/campagne-la-nuit-4-s1880.html, CC0 (public domain). 50 seconds from 1:40,
low rumble removed, levelled, its end crossfaded into its start to make a 45-second loop (half a second extra each end).
